Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Guide Your Life collects the following information you voluntarily provide:

  • Birth information: date, time, and city of birth
  • Gender: as self-reported (optional)
  • MBTI type: as self-selected (optional)
  • Account data: email and password (if you create an account)

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Generating your personalized personality profile ("Soul Portrait")
  • Calculating daily, weekly, and monthly insights
  • Improving the accuracy of our analysis engine

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to any third parties.

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or third-party cookies.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored on encrypted servers
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
  • All communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Birth information is processed and stored separately from account data

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Delete — request complete deletion of your data
  • Export — download your profile data in JSON format
  • Withdraw consent — delete your account at any time

6. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have additional rights:

  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

Legal basis for processing: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

7. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

8. Children's Privacy

Guide Your Life is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.
