Frequently Asked

Is this scientifically accurate?
Soul Cosmos blends evidence-based psychology (Big Five, MBTI) with traditional systems (astrology, Zi Wei, I Ching). We don't claim scientific proof — we offer a multi-dimensional framework for self-reflection that many find surprisingly insightful.
How is this different from Co-Star or The Pattern?
Those apps focus on Western astrology alone. Soul Cosmos fuses 7 systems — including Eastern traditions like Zi Wei Dou Shu and I Ching — and uses AI to cross-validate insights across all of them. The result is a portrait no single system could produce.
Do I need to know my exact birth time?
Birth time improves accuracy for your Rising sign and house placements, but it's not required. If you don't know it, we use noon as a default and note which insights may shift with a different time.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your birth info is used only to generate your profile. We don't sell data or share it with third parties. You can delete your account and all data at any time.
